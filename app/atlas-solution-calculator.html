About ATLaS

Solution Calculator is the first part of ATLaS, a toolkit for preparing laboratory solutions. This version includes percent solutions, molar solutions, dilution, preparation from concentrated reagents, unit conversion, concentration conversions and serial dilution.

How to use it

Choose a calculator, enter your values and units, then select Calculate. Load example fills a worked example. Editing an input clears the old result. You can copy a result, save a text record or print it. History lists every successful calculation; Copy all copies their complete records.

Your entries and calculation history stay in memory for this session. Closing or reloading the file clears them. The application makes no network requests. The reference links below open online only when you select them.

Calculation basis

  • Volumes refer to the final solution volume, unless a field explicitly says stock volume.
  • % w/v means grams per 100 mL of final solution. % v/v means millilitres per 100 mL of final solution. % w/w means grams per 100 g of final solution.
  • A mass assay is the mass fraction of the stated chemical, not a chromatography area percentage. A 100% assay is the default for solid calculations.
  • Volume-based dilution uses concentration × volume conservation. % w/w dilution uses concentration × total mass instead.
  • Results display up to six significant digits. This is display precision; your input measurements determine experimental precision.
